Many solar projects in Panama leave money on the table for an avoidable reason: they are
designed only for peak solar hours. There is a way to capture the rest — under the permit
you already have.

## The peak-hour bottleneck

Picture the power grid as a highway. At midday, when the sun is strongest, every solar
plant "merges onto the road" at once. The highway congests, the energy price drops, and
your most valuable production sells cheap.

> A battery system is your alternate lane: it stores energy when the highway is full and
> releases it once the road clears.

## What storage changes

In concrete terms: a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) integrated into your plant
captures the peak-hour surplus and dispatches it off-peak, when energy is worth more and
the grid has capacity. All within the limits of your current permit — without renegotiating
the essentials.
