For a company like yours, the website should not primarily sell "solar" or "wind". It should sell **certainty, execution capability, regulatory expertise, and lifecycle project delivery**.

Most renewable energy firms in Latin America build websites that look like engineering portfolios. The stronger positioning is to present yourselves as the company that takes a project from concept to operation and keeps it performing for decades.

## Marketing Objectives

Your website should help visitors answer:

1. Can these people deliver a project?
2. Can they navigate Panama's regulatory environment?
3. Have they done similar projects before?
4. Can they reduce my risk?
5. How do I contact them for a proposal?

The site should support multiple audiences:

* Project developers
* Investors and funds
* Commercial & industrial energy users
* Utilities
* EPC partners
* Government entities
* Landowners
* Engineering firms

---

If your goal is to position yourselves as one of Panama's leading renewable energy project firms, the strongest structure is:

**Home → Services → Projects → Industries → Insights → Contact**

That sequence mirrors how sophisticated clients evaluate engineering and energy companies: first capability, then proof, then relevance to their sector, then expertise. The website should feel less like an engineering company brochure and more like the digital presence of a project delivery platform.
